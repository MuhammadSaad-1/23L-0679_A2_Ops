======================================================================
           TASK 2.7: HYPERPARAMETER TUNING DISCUSSION
======================================================================

1. Hyperparameter That Mattered Most:
   The learning rate had the largest impact on convergence and stability.
   Setting LR too high (e.g. 1e-3) with unfrozen backbone layers caused weight
   distortion ('catastrophic forgetting'), whereas moderate rates (1e-4 to 5e-4)
   provided steady and smooth gradient descent.

2. Why Selecting the Best Validation Score Can Overfit the Validation Set:
   Every time we evaluate a model configuration on the validation set and use
   its validation score to pick the 'best' hyperparameters, we effectively leak
   information from the validation set into our meta-decision process. Over many
   trials, random statistical variance in the validation samples allows
   configurations that coincidentally fit the idiosyncratic noise of that specific
   validation split to score highest, even if they generalize poorly. This is why
   a completely held-out, untouched TEST set is mandatory for final unbiased
   performance evaluation.
